description: "These jumbo Smores Cookies combine everything you love about classic chocolate chip cookies and campfire smores into one insanely delicious dessert that is somehow better than both. Instead of simply tossing a few marshmallows into cookie dough, these cookies are made with a thick and chewy graham cracker cookie base packed with chopped chocolate and mini marshmallows. They are topped with a giant marshmallow torched to perfection and finished with a drizzle of dark chocolate.

This recipe is part of a better than Crumbl cookie series. The flavor balances the warmth of graham crackers, the sweetness of marshmallows, and the richness of chocolate. The texture features crispy golden edges with a chewy, gooey center that is fully baked but remains beautifully soft.

THE THREE CORE COMPONENTS
1. The Graham Cracker Cookie Base: Positioned texturally between a snickerdoodle and a sugar cookie, this dough incorporates crushed graham crackers to establish the foundational smores flavor. It is filled with mini marshmallows and dark chocolate pieces to create crispy edges and a chewy center.
2. Giant Marshmallows: Each cookie features a giant marshmallow on top that becomes gooey and warm during the final minutes of baking, completed with a blowtorch for a toasted finish.
3. Dark Chocolate Drizzle: A generous finish of melted dark chocolate completes the smores profile. Crushed graham crackers can also be added on top as an optional garnish.

MARSHMALLOW SELECTION INSIGHTS
The author uses Ziyad brand mini and giant marshmallows, which are Halal and available online. Regular marshmallows like Jet Puffed work well too. Vegetarian alternatives made with carrageenan instead of gelatin like the Trader Joes version can be substituted, but they do not melt or stretch with the same elasticity as traditional gelatin based options.

STRUCTURAL STRATEGY FOR GIANT MARSHMALLOWS
Standard methods like placing giant marshmallows at the bottom or stuffing them inside the dough tend to compromise the structural integrity of the cookie. The most successful technique involves adding the giant marshmallow to the top of the cookie near the very end of the baking window. This allows the marshmallow to become toasted and gooey without causing the cookie base to collapse or distort. Mini marshmallows are safe to mix directly into the dough since they do not disrupt the shape.

THE THREE STEP BAKING ARCHITECTURE
To achieve the ideal combination of a crispy exterior and a chewy center, the recipe uses a non traditional three step temperature adjustment:
1. Initial Setting: Bake at 190 degrees Celsius for 8 minutes to firmly set the cookie edges.
2. Internal Cooking: Drop the oven temperature to 175 degrees Celsius and bake for 7 more minutes to cook the center thoroughly.
3. Marshmallow Integration: Remove the tray briefly to press an indentation into the center, insert the giant marshmallow, and return to the oven at 175 degrees Celsius for 2 to 4 minutes.

The author advises against multitasking during this window to ensure the timing transitions are executed perfectly.

CRITICAL BAKING TIPS
Baking Sheet Color: Always use a light colored baking sheet. Dark sheets absorb too much heat and cause the bottoms of the cookies to over brown.
The Test Cookie Rule: Baking a single test cookie before the main batch is highly emphasized. Because individual oven calibrations vary widely, a test run is the only reliable way to pinpoint the exact required baking duration.
Visual Doneness Over Time: Trust visual cues rather than the clock alone. Perfectly baked cookies display a golden hue and set edges while maintaining a soft center. They require about 20 minutes of cooling to firm up properly.
Bottom Browning Mitigation: If the cookie bottoms are browning too rapidly, you can slide a second, cold baking sheet directly underneath the active cookie sheet at the exact moment you lower the oven temperature from 190 degrees Celsius to 175 degrees Celsius. This buffers the heat transfer and slows down the browning.

COMPONENT PREPARATION TIPS
Ingredient Weight Accuracy: Always use a kitchen scale to measure ingredients. Items like flour and crushed graham crackers cannot be measured consistently using volume cups.
Sticky Dough Management: If the dough feels overly soft or sticky during handling, place it in the refrigerator for 10 to 15 minutes before attempting to shape the portions.
Chilling Discipline: Do not shortcut the chilling phase. The portioned dough requires a minimum of 6 hours of refrigeration, though overnight is ideal. Skipping this step causes the cookies to spread excessively, destroying the thick texture.

STORAGE AND PRESERVATION
Room Temperature and Refrigeration: Cookies are best enjoyed warm when the components are fully melted. Leftovers should be stored in an airtight container for up to 2 days at room temperature or up to 5 days in the refrigerator. Reheat them in an oven at 175 degrees Celsius.
Freezing Strategy: Freezing the raw cookie dough balls is preferred over freezing baked cookies. They keep well for up to 3 months. They can be baked directly from a frozen state by adding an extra 3 to 4 minutes to the internal cooking stage before applying the giant marshmallows."

Add @unsalted butter{12%tbsp}(room temperature) , @granulated sugar{1/3%cup} , and @light brown sugar{2/3%cup}(packed) to a #bowl{} and whisk on medium high speed for ~{2-3%minutes} until the mixture turns fluffy and light in color. Keep scraping the bowl along the way.

Add @large egg{1} , @egg yolk{1} , and @vanilla{1 1/2%tsp}. Whisk for another ~{1%minute} until a creamy mixture forms.

Add @crushed graham crackers{1/2%cup} and whisk until just combined.

Now sift @all purpose flour{1 3/4%cups} , @baking powder{3/4%tsp} , @baking soda{1/4%tsp} , and @salt{1/2%tsp} in a separate bowl and mix to combine. Add the dry ingredients to the cookie dough in 2 additions, and use a rubber spatula to gently fold until the flour is almost mixed into the dough.

Add @dark chocolate chips{1/2%cup}(or chopped chocolate) and @mini marshmallows{1/2%cup} , and continue folding until no more streaks of flour remain and the add-ins are evenly dispersed. Don't overmix.

The cookie dough should be soft and easy to work with. If it feels too sticky, refrigerate it for ~{10-15%minutes} until it becomes easier to shape with your hands.

Divide the cookie dough into 5 portions, each should be about 5.5oz in weight. Working one piece at a time, gently shape into a ball, then lightly flatten so they form a 2.5-3" disc. Repeat with all portions.

Place the cookie dough balls on a plate/tray and refrigerate overnight (minimum ~{6%hours}, overnight recommended).

When you're ready to bake after chilling, pre-heat the oven to 375F (conventional / no fan) and line a large light colored #baking sheet{} with parchment paper.

Place the chilled cookie dough balls on the baking sheet, keeping at least 3" between each.

Transfer to the oven and bake at 375F for ~{8%minutes}, then drop the temperature to 350F and bake for another ~{7%minutes}.

Remove the tray from the oven, and use the back of a measuring spoon to press down the middles of the cookies and create a pocket/indent. Place @giant marshmallows{5} into the pocket on each cookie, and quickly return the cookie tray to the oven. Try to do this step as quickly as you can so the cookies remain hot.

Bake for another ~{2-4%minutes} until the cookies are golden, look set on the edges but still soft in the middle, and the marshmallows on top have started to get a light golden color on them.

Remove the cookies from the oven, and let them cool at room temperature for ~{20%minutes}.

In the meanwhile, melt @dark chocolate{50%g} in the microwave in ~{20%seconds} increments, stirring in between. Transfer it to a piping bag and snip a small hole at the tip.

Once the cookies have rested, take a blowtorch and quickly torch the marshmallows so they become warm and toasted. Then drizzle the chocolate on top of the cookies generously.

Serve warm and enjoy!
