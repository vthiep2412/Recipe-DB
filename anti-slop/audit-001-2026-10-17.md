## Detailed Resolutions Applied

### Finding 01 — Resolved
* **Change**: Updated Section 3.3 to state that client canvas downscales photos to a high-quality blob, and the Cloudinary upload preset applies an incoming transformation (`f_jxl,q_90`) to immediately transcode and store the asset as a lossy JPEG XL master.
* **Result**: Eliminates the format gap between client canvas capabilities and Cloudinary master storage.

### Finding 02 — Resolved
* **Change**: Replaced `N/A` in the Section 5 budget table with explicit credit calculations (~0.00005 credits per 50 KB photo, ~0.0006 credits per 600 KB clip).
* **Result**: Grounded numbers replace generic placeholders.

### Finding 03 — Resolved
* **Change**: Updated Phase 1 to explicitly restrict the unsigned Cloudinary preset to target folders (`recipes/images/` and `recipes/videos/`), max file size caps (5 MB photos, 35 MB clips), and allowed media formats.
* **Result**: Prevents arbitrary file uploads and quota drainage.
